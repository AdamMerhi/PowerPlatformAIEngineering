2. Assess the claim (Advisor)

Retrieves the policy clauses relevant to this claim, then the model decides and cites them. Repeat calls are served from the stored result.

3. Check a decision (Verifier)

Independently re-checks a specialist's decision against the PDS. A disagreement means: requires approval by line manager.

Each check is stored as a Decision Check row.

4. Ask the policy

Answers only from the closest policy passages, with citations. If the documents do not say, it says so.
